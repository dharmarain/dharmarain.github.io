## Who we are

We are a relaxed community for the study and practice of all three vehicles of Buddhism. The community here focuses more on scholastic study, and also engages with philosophy and other traditions.

We don't require members to be Buddhists, and are welcoming to all traditions and beliefs and are willing to respectfully discuss things with everyone.

═══════════════ ❖ ═══════════════

What the community offers:

1. Books and other resources
2. Relaxed discussion channels
3. Silent meditation and study channels
4. Watercooler for fun topics

We try to keep noise to a minimum so we are intentionally keeping smaller number of channels.

═══════════════ ❖ ═══════════════

Please note that to access the server you need to send an application and wait until someone approves you. But we generally don't reject any applications that are sent in good faith and sincerity.
